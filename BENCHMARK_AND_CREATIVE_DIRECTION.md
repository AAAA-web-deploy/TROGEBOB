## Creative selection

**Benchmark APU's *community-meme-world* principle, not literal visuals or source code.** The previous $3OR1 designs followed the usual nav→neon banner→3 story cards→5 plain grids. That is intentionally rejected here. The new format is an **analog meme-zine / chaotic playground**:

- Opening: oversized cutout mascot, spontaneous stickers, wobbly comic typography, acid yellow and pink over cream paper, deliberately asymmetric placement.
- Scrollable accident comic, not dry prose or a formal timeline. It is specifically Solana first and Ethereum second.
- Playable Face Lab with 3 sets of portraits + 1 live result, with meaningful downloadable images. Users participate on arrival, rather than hunting for buttons on a token landing page.
- Hand-pasted polaroid meme wall, irregular card sizing, image-led rather than paragraph-led.
- Short explanatory footer with contract fields disabled until there is real data.

## Assets / visual provenance

The user previously provided the official working meme-art references and many derivatives they approved. This package uses those images as *source material*; the fifteen input portraits, five illustrated same-emotion panels, five illustrated mixed panels, four individual story scenes, five gallery pictures, and hero mascot were newly cropped, separated, compressed and repackaged into independently usable WebP assets. Ethereum/Solana vector icons, page background texture, code, interactions and visual collage design were created for this package. The old banner is included for archival consistency but never used as full-width hero.

## The open question

**Vitalik+Anatoly+Raj** is the token name. **$TROGEBOB** is the character/ticker. The selected slogan is `Three faces. One mind—or Three?!`. It is an open comedic question; the site never asserts that a real person, a confirmed Netflix character, or all three founders officially merged. This is independently fictional parody, not proof of licensing or investment merits.
